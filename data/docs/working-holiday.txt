Working Holiday visa (subclass 417)

The Working Holiday visa lets young people from partner countries spend up to 12 months in Australia, working short-term to help fund travel. Applicants must be aged 18 to 30 inclusive (18 to 35 for citizens of Canada, France, Ireland, Italy, Denmark, and a few other countries) at the time of application, and must hold an eligible passport.

Partner countries and regions for the 417 stream include the United Kingdom, Ireland, Germany, France, Italy, the Netherlands, Belgium, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Estonia, Cyprus, Malta, Canada, the Republic of Korea, Japan, Taiwan, and Hong Kong. The list is set by reciprocal agreements and changes from time to time.

Other requirements:

- Sufficient funds, typically AUD 5,000, plus a return or onward ticket or funds to buy one.
- No accompanying dependent children.
- Health, character, and insurance requirements.

A second year visa (417 second) can be applied for after completing 88 days (3 months) of specified work in regional Australia in eligible industries such as agriculture, fishing, construction, and mining.

A third year visa (417 third) can be applied for after completing 6 months of specified regional work while on the second year visa.

Work for a single employer is normally limited to 6 months, though exceptions apply in agriculture and some northern Australia industries.

The visa cannot lead directly to permanent residence, but time spent on a 417 is commonly used to transition into a 482 or 500 visa.
