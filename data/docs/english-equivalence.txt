English language test equivalence for Australian visas

Most skilled, sponsored, graduate, and student visas require evidence of English language ability. Home Affairs accepts several tests and groups results into bands.

Functional English: typically required for secondary applicants in some streams. Equivalent to IELTS overall 4.5, or an average PTE Academic of 30.

Vocational English: IELTS 5 in each of the four components, PTE Academic 36 in each, TOEFL iBT 12 listening / 13 reading / 18 writing / 18 speaking, or OET grade C in each component. Used for some trade and graduate streams.

Competent English: the baseline required to apply for points-tested skilled visas (subclasses 189, 190, 491) and the temporary graduate visa (485). IELTS 6 each band, PTE Academic 50 each, TOEFL iBT 12L/13R/21W/18S, OET grade B.

Proficient English: IELTS 7 each band, PTE Academic 65 each, TOEFL iBT 24L/24R/27W/23S, OET grade B. Earns 10 points in the skilled points test.

Superior English: IELTS 8 each band, PTE Academic 79 each, TOEFL iBT 28L/29R/30W/26S, OET grade A. Earns 20 points in the skilled points test.

Some passport holders (UK, Ireland, US, Canada, New Zealand) are exempt from providing test results for English requirements. Test results must usually be no more than three years old at the time of application.
