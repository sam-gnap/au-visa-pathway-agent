Work and Holiday visa (subclass 462)

The Work and Holiday visa is similar to the Working Holiday (417) but applies to a different group of partner countries and is subject to annual country caps. Applicants must be 18 to 30 inclusive at time of application and hold an eligible passport.

Partner countries for the 462 stream include the United States, Argentina, Chile, Brazil, Peru, Ecuador, Uruguay, China, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Malaysia, Singapore, the Philippines, India, Mongolia, Israel, Türkiye, Spain, Portugal, Poland, Hungary, Greece, Slovenia, Slovakia, Austria, Czech Republic, Croatia, Switzerland, Luxembourg, Papua New Guinea, and several Pacific Island and South-East Asian nations.

Most 462 countries (other than the US) have an annual cap on first-year places. Caps fill quickly when they open each program year (1 July). Some countries also require additional documents such as a letter of support from the home government, evidence of tertiary education, and functional English.

Other requirements:

- Sufficient funds (typically AUD 5,000) plus a return or onward ticket.
- No accompanying dependent children.
- Health, character, and insurance requirements.

Second-year extensions require 3 months (88 days) of specified work in northern Australia or in critical sectors in regional areas. Third-year extensions require a further 6 months of specified work on a second-year 462.

Like the 417, work for a single employer is generally limited to 6 months. The 462 does not lead directly to PR but is commonly used as a step toward study or skilled visas.
