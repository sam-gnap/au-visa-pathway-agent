Student visa (subclass 500)

The Student visa lets non-citizens stay in Australia to undertake a CRICOS-registered course of study. The visa lasts for the duration of the course plus a short buffer.

Eligibility requires enrolment in a CRICOS-registered course at an Australian education provider, evidenced by a Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE). Eligible course types include schools, English language intensive courses (ELICOS), vocational education and training (VET), higher education, postgraduate research, and non-award courses.

Genuine Student (GS) requirement: replaces the former Genuine Temporary Entrant (GTE) test from 2024. Applicants must demonstrate that they genuinely intend to study and that the course is appropriate for their background and career goals. Decision makers consider study history, immigration history, financial circumstances, and current ties.

Financial requirements: applicants must show evidence of funds to cover the first 12 months of tuition, living costs, and travel. From mid-2024 the indicative living-costs figure for a single applicant is AUD 29,710 per year, with additional amounts for partners and children.

English: required at the level set by the provider, with minimum scores depending on course type (e.g. IELTS 5.5 overall for most higher education, lower for packaged ELICOS).

Health insurance (OSHC) is mandatory for the full visa period.

Work conditions: student visa holders may work up to 48 hours per fortnight during course sessions and unlimited hours during scheduled course breaks. Postgraduate research students have no fortnightly work cap once their course has commenced.
