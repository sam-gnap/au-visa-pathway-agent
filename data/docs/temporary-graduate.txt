Temporary Graduate visa (subclass 485)

The Temporary Graduate visa lets recent graduates of Australian institutions live, work, and study in Australia after finishing their studies. From mid-2024 it has two main streams.

Post-Higher Education Work stream (replaces the former Post-Study Work stream): for graduates of bachelor's, master's, or doctoral degrees from an Australian institution. Duration depends on qualification level:

- Bachelor's or master's by coursework: 2 years.
- Master's by research: 3 years.
- Doctorate: 4 years.

Graduates of regional institutions may be eligible for an additional 1 to 2 years if they live in designated regional areas while on the visa.

Post-Vocational Education Work stream (replaces the former Graduate Work stream): for graduates of trade qualifications or other vocational qualifications relevant to occupations on the relevant skilled list. Duration is generally 18 months, with extensions for regional graduates.

Common eligibility requirements across both streams:

- Aged under 35 at the time of application (50 for Hong Kong/British National Overseas passport holders).
- Held a student visa in the last 6 months.
- Completed a CRICOS-registered course of at least 2 academic years (92 weeks) in Australia.
- Competent English (e.g. IELTS 6 each band).
- Health insurance for the full visa period.
- Health and character requirements.

The 485 is commonly used as a bridge to skilled or sponsored permanent visas such as the 482, 186, 189, 190, or 491.
